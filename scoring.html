How Scoring Works

Difficulty Score

The difficulty score is based on the value of how hard each of the skill you have in your routine are added together and usually added onto from a base of 12.0.

Execution Score

This is your difficulty or starting value and what is taken from it because of deductions. This overall measures how well a gymnast preformed their routine and evaluating their technique and form.

How Routines Are Judged

  1. In high level gymnastics there are usually more than one judge in order to make sure nothing in the routine is missed or calculated incorrectly
  2. There are judges that calculate the difficulty score being the values of the skills in the routine. And those that watch the execution which are the ones that take points away for any mistakes that may be made
  3. It is then decided if their are any neutral deductions and then all those values are put together to then give the gymnast their final score
